Eligibility assessment

Probation Eligibility Assessment

Tell us about your sentence and where you are in it. An attorney reviews what you send and tells you whether an early termination motion is worth filing now, later, or not at all.

This form does not decide anything

Nothing here scores your answers or tells you whether you qualify. Eligibility for early termination is a legal judgement that depends on your sentencing documents, and it can only be made by an attorney who has read them. Any site that gives you an instant answer is not giving you a reliable one.

Before you start

Submitting this form does not create an attorney-client relationship and does not make what you send confidential. Please give us enough to assess the case and no more — do not include details you would not want read by someone who has not yet been able to check for a conflict of interest. If your matter is urgent, particularly if a warrant may have been issued, contact us directly rather than waiting on a form.
