Can I get a bond on a violation of probation?

Not automatically. A violation warrant is frequently issued without bond, and setting one usually requires a motion and a hearing.

What does the state have to prove?

That the violation was both willful and substantial, by a preponderance of the evidence. Hearsay alone is not enough to revoke.

How long does a violation hearing take to schedule?

It varies by county. Scheduling practice differs between circuits, which is covered on each location page.

Containers and spacing

Containers

.container-narrow 760px · .container-page 1200px · .container-wide 1360px. All centre themselves and carry responsive inline padding.

Section spacing

.section-sm 40/56px · .section 64/96px · .section-lg 80/128px, mobile then desktop. Tint with .section-mist; reserve .section-navy for compact bands.
