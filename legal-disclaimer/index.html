This is information, not advice

FloridaProbationLaw.com explains how probation works in Florida in general terms. It cannot tell you what will happen in your case, because that depends on your sentence, your record, the county, and the judge.

No attorney-client relationship

Using this site or contacting Hoffman Legal through it does not make us your attorneys. Until a conflict check has been completed and representation confirmed in writing, we cannot advise you and what you send is not confidential.

No outcome is promised

Whether probation is terminated early rests entirely within the court’s discretion. Nothing on this site promises, predicts or implies any particular result, and no attorney can honestly offer one.

No case results or testimonials

This site publishes no success rates, client testimonials or past case results. Past outcomes would not predict yours in any event, since every case turns on its own facts.

Content pending attorney review

Pages summarising Florida statutes carry a visible notice where the summary has not been checked against the official text. Those notices are accurate: treat the marked pages as orientation and read the statute itself.

Florida state supervision only

This site concerns probation and community control imposed by Florida state courts. Federal supervised release is a separate system with different statutes, courts and procedures. Nothing here applies to it.

If you need advice

Speak to an attorney licensed in Florida about your own case. If a violation warrant may have been issued, do so now rather than after an arrest — see violations.
