Early Termination of Probation in Hillsborough County

Hillsborough is its own judicial circuit at the centre of a metro area that spills into four neighbouring counties. Crossing a county line here is an ordinary part of the day — and that has consequences while you are under supervision.

Ending Hillsborough probation early

Tampa Bay works as one labour market across several counties. People live in Pinellas and work in Tampa, or live in Pasco and commute south, without thinking about county lines from one week to the next.

Supervision makes those lines matter again. It is worth being precise about two separate things: which county's court holds your case, and what your own conditions say about leaving the county. The first determines where a motion goes. The second determines whether your commute needs authorising.

The Thirteenth Judicial Circuit

Hillsborough County is the entirety of the Thirteenth Judicial Circuit, with Tampa as its county seat. No neighbouring county shares it.

Every county around it sits in a different circuit — Pinellas to the west, Pasco to the north, Polk to the east and Manatee to the south. For a metropolitan area this interconnected, that means a short drive can take you into another circuit entirely, and a case in one is not administered alongside a case in another.

Filing and hearing, in general terms

The motion is filed in the existing criminal case in the court that imposed the sentence, the State is served, and the judge rules on the papers or sets a hearing. The general sequence is set out in the process, and what drives the elapsed time in the timeline.

Eligibility considerations

Statutory factors, unchanged by county and covered in full on the early termination guide: term served, conditions complete, restitution and costs satisfied, no pending violation, and nothing in the offense or plea agreement that restricts the request. See eligibility.

The local risk worth naming is a technical one. In a region where people cross county lines daily, an unapproved trip is an easy way to acquire a violation without intending to — and a pending violation stops an early termination request outright. See violations and reasons for denial.

Related probation matters

Travel permission deserves particular attention in Tampa Bay. If your conditions restrict leaving the county and your job is across the bay, that is worth resolving properly — either with a standing arrangement or through a modification — rather than hoping it does not come up.

Where the term is not far enough along for discharge, administrative probation reduces the reporting burden without ending supervision. Moving to a neighbouring county still requires approval: see relocation.

Nearby areas

Hillsborough borders Pinellas, Pasco, Polk and Manatee. Of the counties we currently have pages for, the nearest is Orange, across Central Florida.

The surrounding Tampa Bay counties do not have pages yet. That is a statement about what has been written, not about whether a case there can be reviewed, and it is not a claim about offices. Availability of representation depends on the case and its location. The locations index shows the counties covered so far.

Hillsborough questions

I live in Pinellas and was sentenced in Hillsborough. Where does the motion go?

Back to Hillsborough, as the sentencing county. Pinellas is a different circuit, so although the drive is short, the two courts are administratively separate.

Does my daily commute across the bay count as leaving the county?

It depends entirely on the conditions in your own order, and this is not a question to guess at — an unapproved trip can be treated as a technical violation regardless of how routine it feels. Read the conditions, and raise a recurring commute with your officer.

Will a Hillsborough judge set a hearing, or rule on the papers?

Practice differs by division and by judge, and we are not going to characterise this circuit's without verifying it. Whether the State objects is usually the biggest single factor anywhere in Florida.

Is there a Tampa office?

Not claimed here. Hoffman Legal's office location appears on the contact page, and whether representation is available in a particular county depends on the case and its location.
