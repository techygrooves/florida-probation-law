Who this applies to

This policy covers FloridaProbationLaw.com, which is operated by Hoffman Legal, PLLC. It does not cover any other website you may reach from a link here.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form on this site: your name, contact details, the county your case is in, and whatever you choose to tell us about your situation. There is no account system and nothing asks you to register.

This site uses no advertising trackers and sets no third-party marketing cookies. Submitting a form does transmit what you typed to the form service named below. If analytics are added later they will be privacy-respecting and cookieless, and this page will say so before they go live.

How a form submission reaches us

Forms on this site are delivered by Formspree, a third-party form service. What you type is transmitted to Formspree, which forwards it to the firm and retains a copy on its own systems under its own privacy policy and retention rules. We name it because it means your enquiry passes through a company that is not this firm before anyone here reads it.

If that is not acceptable to you, call (954) 459-4236 or email david@hoffman.legal instead. Telephone enquiries are accepted 24/7 and reach the firm without an intermediary.

What we do with it

We use it to respond to you and to assess whether we can help. We do not sell it, and we do not pass it to lead-generation services or other firms.

It is not confidential until it is

This is the part that matters most. Information sent through this website is not protected by attorney-client privilege until an attorney-client relationship exists, which requires a conflict check and a written agreement. Please send enough to identify the matter and no more — see the contact page.

How long we keep it

Retention period to be confirmed by Hoffman Legal. Enquiries that do not become matters are kept only as long as needed to run a conflict check and respond.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask us to delete it, subject to any professional obligation to retain records. Contact us to do so.
